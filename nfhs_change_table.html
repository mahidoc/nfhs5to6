Sources: IIPS, NFHS‑5 India Fact Sheet (2019–21) and NFHS‑6 India Fact Sheet (2023–24), Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India. NFHS‑5 column taken from the NFHS‑5 fact sheet; NFHS‑6 column from the NFHS‑6 fact sheet.

Colour logic: shifts are coded by their public-health direction, not merely by sign — e.g. a rise in caesarean rate or in overweight/obesity is shaded unfavourable, while a fall in stunting or anaemia is favourable. Demographic or care-mix shifts with no inherent "good" direction (fertility at replacement, public–private facility share, sterilization mix) are shown as neutral.
